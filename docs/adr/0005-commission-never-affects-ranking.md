# Commission never affects ranking

Normal results are ordered only by how well they match a Shopper's search and UK size; whether a Retailer pays Yxra commission never moves them up or down. Placements are the only paid route to prominence, and they are always labelled Sponsored. A reader might expect an affiliate site to favour paying Retailers; we deliberately don't, so Shoppers can trust the results and the ASA has nothing to object to.
