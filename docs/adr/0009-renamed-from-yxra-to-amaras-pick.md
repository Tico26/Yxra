# Renamed from Yxra to Amara's Pick

Decided by Tico on 27 Oct 2026, before launch and before any Awin applications went out.

The site, its logo, its web address (amaraspick.co.uk) and every name behind it changed from Yxra to Amara's Pick: the Cloudflare Workers (amaras-pick-web, amaras-pick-api), the repository settings (AMARAS_PICK_*), the collector's user agent (AmarasPick/1.0) and these docs. Older docs and decisions were reworded to the new name rather than kept as they were. The look stays the black and white magazine design, with the logo redrawn as AMARA'S | PICK in Didot.

Amara is the site's friendly guide: the site speaks of her by name and says what she found ("Amara checks every shop each night"), never what she likes. She never hand-picks or endorses items, so ranking still follows [ADR 0004](0004-placements-obey-the-same-rules.md) and [ADR 0005](0005-commission-never-affects-ranking.md).

The old address /how-yxra-makes-money redirects to /how-amaras-pick-makes-money. Until the AMARAS_PICK_* repository variables are added, the workflows fall back to the old YXRA_* ones for the contact address and trader details.
